# Chinese (Singapore) glossary and style guide

**For:** translators and the native-speaker reviewer of the 中文 version of thecharismacode.co
**Language:** Simplified Chinese as written in Singapore (简体中文，新加坡用语)
**Status:** first draft, 4 Nov 2026. Every term here can be changed by the reviewer; change it here first, then in the pages.

## 1. Style rules

1. **Simplified characters, Singapore usage.** Prefer the words Singaporean readers see in Lianhe Zaobao and on local corporate sites, not mainland internet slang. Avoid 赋能, 小伙伴, 打卡, 亲, 爆款, 年会 (use 年终晚宴), 复盘 (use 总结回顾).
2. **Address the reader as 您**, the studio as 我们. Warm and confident, never hard-sell.
3. **Translate the meaning, not word for word.** Short, natural sentences. Keep every claim exactly as strong as the English: add no new facts, numbers or promises.
4. **No guarantees, no "celebrity".** Never 保证, 百分之百, 名人司仪, 明星司仪 (client decision, 23 Oct 2026).
5. **Languages: only English and Mandarin.** Never mention Malay or Bahasa (decision 22 Oct 2026).
6. **Punctuation:** full-width Chinese punctuation （，。：；！？“”）. **No dashes** in visible text (no ——, –, —); use 至 for ranges: 20 至 50 人, 11 月至 3 月.
7. **Spacing:** one half-width space between Chinese and Latin letters or numbers: 超过 15 年, S$588 起, WhatsApp 联系德忠, 在 Instagram 关注我们. No space before or after full-width punctuation.
8. **Numbers and prices:** Arabic numerals where the English uses digits (15 年, 20 至 50 人); a number the English spells out as a word may become a Chinese numeral (five years → 五年, three steps → 三个步骤). Prices unchanged in S$: S$588 起, 每位参与者 S$88 起, 每节 S$120; ranges S$588 至 S$1,288. Dates: 2025 年 12 月.
9. **Keep in English:** URLs, phone numbers, email addresses, @handles, The Charisma Code, client company names (Lectrix Engineering, The Banyan Group, SOMA Fragrances, Sheraton), venue names without a well-known Chinese name (Mandai Rainforest Resort).
10. **Client reviews stay in their original English.** They are real people's words (Google reviews). Translate only the source label: `Google review` → `Google 评价`. De Zhong's own quotes are translated.
