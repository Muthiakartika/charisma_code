## Section 7 — How booking works

**H2 ·** How our event planners run your event

No two events are the same, so no two plans are either. This is how we take yours from brief to the night itself.

**H3 ·** 1. Your objectives

What should change, or be celebrated, because of this event? We start with the outcome you want.

**H3 ·** 2. Your people

Who will be in the room: their roles, languages, energy and how well they already know each other.

**H3 ·** 3. Format and flow

We plan the programme, run of show and timing around your venue, headcount and schedule.

**H3 ·** 4. Budget and logistics

We source the venue and trusted vendors, from AV to photography, within your budget.

**H3 ·** 5. Delivery and debrief

We run the event and host it on stage, then review the results with you afterwards.

## Section 14 — FAQ

**H2 ·** Event planning questions

**H3 ·** How much does a corporate event cost in Singapore?

It depends on headcount, venue, programme and vendors. Share your budget range in the enquiry form and we will recommend a plan that fits it; every proposal itemises venue, vendors and hosting.

**H3 ·** What size of events do you plan?

From a single department to company-wide events of several hundred guests.

**H3 ·** Can you find and book the venue?

Yes. We recommend venues that fit your headcount, date and budget, and handle the booking with you.

**H3 ·** Can you host the event as well as plan it?

Yes. Our founder, De Zhong, emcees in English and Mandarin, so planning and hosting sit with one team.

**H3 ·** Do you plan family days?

Yes. Games, food, emcee and logistics in one plan, indoors or outdoors.

**H3 ·** Do you plan dinner and dances?

Yes. Our D&D planning page has the detail, from theme to the grand draw.

**H3 ·** Can you plan a product launch?

Yes. Our product launch events page covers planning, hosting, creators and content.

**H3 ·** How far in advance should we enquire?

As early as you can, especially for year-end and festive periods when dates fill up quickly. Share your preferred dates in the enquiry form and we will confirm availability.

**H3 ·** Will we receive a proposal and quotation?

Yes. Once we understand your objectives and requirements, we prepare a tailored proposal with a recommended programme and a quotation.
