## Website developer — SEO and back-end instructions

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Primary keyword | none of its own: proof page for every service |
| Secondary keywords | Supports the service pages; each case study's own URL can rank for its client and venue names. |
| SEO title | Our Work \| Corporate Events, D&D, Weddings & Campaigns  (54 characters) |
| Meta description | Case studies from The Charisma Code: company dinners, Christmas parties, weddings, youth programmes and brand launches in Singapore.  (132 characters) |
| URL | Keep /our-work |
| H1 | Our work  One H1 only. |
| H2 structure | Case studies · Find work by service · What clients say · Planning something similar? |
| Heading rule | The page's main keyword appears in at most two H2s; the rest are plain language. No paragraph or bullet repeats its own heading. |
| Schema | CollectionPage with an ItemList of the case studies. Reviews on the page are not marked up as the business's own AggregateRating (Google treats self-hosted reviews of your own business as ineligible for review stars). Each case study page: Article with author Person. |
| Internal links | Every case study, and the matching service page for each. |

## Page layout instructions

Keep the hero, filter and case-study grid. Add: a by-service list linking each case to its service page; reviews mixed across services; trust ticks; final band. Case-study title pattern (decided 24 Oct 2026): "<Client> <Event> | Case Study".

## Mobile conversion requirements

Sticky bar: **LET'S TALK | WHATSAPP**. Large tap targets; WhatsApp opens the app with the pre-filled message; the phone number is tap-to-call. Paragraphs of two or three lines; the price line is visible in the hero.

## Form back-end

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Email subject | NEW ENQUIRY FROM OUR WORK — [SERVICE] — [DATE] |
| WhatsApp pre-filled message | Hi De Zhong, I saw your work and would like to plan something similar. |
| Spam protection | Invisible captcha or honeypot field; no visible puzzle. |
| Conversion tracking | Form submit, WhatsApp click, phone click and every quote or availability button, each as its own event. |

## Image SEO

| Field | Value |

Real event photos only. WebP or AVIF, compressed. No keyword stuffing in ALT text.

## Wording to avoid

- "Celebrity emcee" (the client's decision, 23 Oct 2026).
- Guarantees of outcomes ("guaranteed", "100%").

## Most important message

Real events for real clients, each linked to the service behind it.
