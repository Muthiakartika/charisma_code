## Section 4 — What a professional emcee does

**H2 ·** What a professional emcee does for your event

A good emcee is the thread that runs through your programme. Most of the work happens before anyone picks up a microphone.

**H3 ·** Before the event

Reviewing the programme and script with you, checking names, titles and pronunciation, and agreeing cues with your venue and AV team.

**H3 ·** On the day

Opening with energy, introducing every speaker correctly, linking segments so there are no awkward gaps, and keeping to time.

**H3 ·** When things change

A late speaker, a technical delay or a programme that runs long is handled on the spot, so guests never feel the pressure.

**H3 ·** For mixed audiences

Moving between English and Mandarin so that every guest follows the speeches, games and announcements.
