## Page layout instructions

Keep the hero, form and the three next steps. Add: four contact cards (WhatsApp, phone, email, address) above the form, the map block with the real address, and a short FAQ. Remove the placeholder address and map before launch if the client has not supplied a real address.

## Mobile conversion requirements

Sticky bar: **WHATSAPP | CALL**. Large tap targets; WhatsApp opens the app with the pre-filled message; the phone number is tap-to-call. Paragraphs of two or three lines; the price line is visible in the hero.

## Form back-end

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Email subject | NEW ENQUIRY FROM CONTACT — [SERVICE] — [DATE] |
| WhatsApp pre-filled message | Hi De Zhong, I'd like to enquire about an event. |
| Spam protection | Invisible captcha or honeypot field; no visible puzzle. |
| Conversion tracking | Form submit, WhatsApp click, phone click and every quote or availability button, each as its own event. |
