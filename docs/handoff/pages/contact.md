## Page layout instructions

Keep the hero, form and the three next steps. Add: three contact cards (WhatsApp, phone, email) above the form, and a short FAQ. No address card and no map.

Sticky bar: **WHATSAPP | CALL**. Large tap targets; WhatsApp opens the app with the pre-filled message; the phone number is tap-to-call. Paragraphs of two or three lines; the price line is visible in the hero.
