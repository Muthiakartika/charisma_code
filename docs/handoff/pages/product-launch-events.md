## Section 2 — What a launch needs

**H2 ·** What every launch has to get right

Guests, media and partners decide in the first few minutes whether your product is worth talking about. These are the moments we plan around.

Invitations, arrivals and a room that already feels like your brand before anyone speaks.

A countdown, a moment of surprise and a clear first look at what is new.

**H3 ·** The message

Speakers introduced properly and key points landed simply, so guests can repeat them afterwards.

**H3 ·** The people in the room

The right media, partners, creators and talent, briefed and in the right place at the right time.

**H3 ·** What happens next

Photos, video and creator posts planned in advance, so the launch keeps working after the lights go down.
