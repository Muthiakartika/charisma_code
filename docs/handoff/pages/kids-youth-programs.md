## Section 2 — Approach

**H2 ·** How we build confidence

The same idea behind everything we do: charisma is not a gift, it is a skill. For young people, it grows in three steps.

**H3 ·** 01 Play first ↓

Games and warm-ups get everyone laughing, so nobody feels put on the spot.

**H3 ·** 02 Practise in a safe space ↓

Every young person gets a turn, with encouragement and clear feedback. Mistakes are part of the fun.

**H3 ·** 03 A moment to shine ↓

Programmes build toward something to be proud of, such as a talk, a performance or a short film.

## Section 3 — Play

**H2 ·** Learning through play

Children learn fastest when they are having fun. Every session starts with games that get them moving, laughing and talking before anyone is asked to stand up and speak.

By the time the real practice starts, the room already feels safe and lively.

- ✓ Games before speeches
- ✓ Movement and laughter
- ✓ Shy children ease in
- ✓ Energy that lasts the session

## Section 4 — Practice

**H2 ·** A safe space to practise speaking

We build up in small steps, from speaking in pairs to small groups to the whole class. Mistakes are part of the fun, and feedback is always kind and specific.

Children learn that nerves are normal, and that they can speak up anyway.

- ✓ Small steps, pairs to whole group
- ✓ Kind, specific feedback
- ✓ Mistakes treated as practice
- ✓ Every child gets a turn

## Section 5 — Showcase

**H2 ·** A moment for every child to shine

Each programme ends with a showcase, a performance or a short talk, so every child finishes on a high in front of an audience.

Parents and teachers see the progress for themselves, and children leave proud of what they did.

- ✓ A showcase at the end
- ✓ Every child takes part
- ✓ Parents and teachers invited
- ✓ Confidence that carries on
