## Guide 1 — Dinner & dance planning checklist

**H2 ·** Dinner & dance planning checklist

**H4 ·** 3 to 6 months before

- Set the date, budget range and headcount. November to March dates go first.
- Shortlist venues that fit your numbers and programme; check their deposit and cut-off dates.
- Book the emcee and any performers.
- Choose a theme and dress code.

**H4 ·** 1 to 2 months before

- Draft the programme: welcome, speeches, dinner, games, lucky draw, finale.
- Confirm AV, photography and decor.
- Decide the lucky draw prizes and the order they are drawn.

**H4 ·** 2 weeks before

- Confirm the final headcount with the venue.
- Send the emcee the run-sheet, speaker names and titles, and the prize list.
- Agree the timing of each segment with the venue and AV team.

**H4 ·** On the night

- Arrive early for the sound check and run-through.
- Keep one person as the contact for the emcee and the venue.
