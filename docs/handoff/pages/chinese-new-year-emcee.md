## Section 8 — Lo hei

**H2 ·** Leading the lo hei

The lo hei is the moment every table stands, tosses the yu sheng and calls out wishes for the year. De Zhong leads it from the stage, saying each auspicious phrase in Mandarin with its meaning in English, so every table knows what to call out and when to toss.

**H4 ·** Phrases we lead with

- 年年有余 (nián nián yǒu yú): abundance year after year
- 大吉大利 (dà jí dà lì): great luck and great profit
- 步步高升 (bù bù gāo shēng): rising higher step by step
- 风生水起 (fēng shēng shuǐ qǐ): good fortune that keeps growing
- 招财进宝 (zhāo cái jìn bǎo): wealth and treasure coming in
- 万事如意 (wàn shì rú yì): may everything go as you wish
