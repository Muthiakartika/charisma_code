## Page layout instructions

Keep the hero and the service grid. The grid becomes twelve cards in a 3 × 4 layout, each with price and a VIEW button (Product Launch Events added; Corporate Workshops shows "Quoted per programme"). Add trust ticks, the quick-match list, five steps, a short FAQ and a final band.

## Mobile conversion requirements

Sticky bar: **LET'S TALK | WHATSAPP**. Large tap targets; WhatsApp opens the app with the pre-filled message; the phone number is tap-to-call. Paragraphs of two or three lines; the price line is visible in the hero.
